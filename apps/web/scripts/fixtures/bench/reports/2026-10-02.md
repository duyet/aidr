## Notes on this run (hand-written)

- **Prod impact.** While the bench ran (about 08:35–09:00 UTC), the z-ai BYOK upstream started returning `upstream_exhausted`: z-ai-coding-byok 429 "Insufficient balance", openrouter-byok 402. In prod `llm_calls`, `z-ai/glm-4.6` review was ok at every call through 08:31, then logged a 404 at 09:01. The bench (7 processes in parallel on the shared key) may have helped drain it. Prod review today is `glm-4.6,stealth/space-bunny-alpha`, and space-bunny failed review 11 of 12 times here.
- Sample sizes: `--limit` 10 (score, jev/decision, cluster, vi-en), 9 (en-vi), 12 (review), 1 (tldr), 50 (topics). Read this as a smoke test, not a ranking.
- Score, topics, review and en-vi ran alone at concurrency 2. Cluster, vi-en and tldr ran in parallel with the others, so their latency includes contention.
- en-vi used `nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b` as the back-translation judge, because GLM was unavailable. Its back-translations are weak: laguna's btRecall is 0.21 here against 0.68 with the glm-4.6 judge in an earlier contended run. Compare en-vi candidates only within one run (same judge).
- `typesafe/jev` called directly returned 404. `anyrouter/decision` reached Jev on 10 of 10 (servedByJev = 1). The other System One deciders (pplx-decider, solar-decide, strands-decider, mercury-decide, kev-4b) returned 404 on this key.
- vi-en: nemotron-3-super and dots answered 10 of 10 (p95 13–15 s). That contradicts the wrangler.toml note that no second concrete id streams JSON reliably. Re-verify before adding either one as a fallback.
- Billed $ is 0 on this key for every model. List $ is the catalog price.
- Cluster F1 is measured against silver `duplicate_of`, and the "other" stories come from the same 12 h window. A low F1 means "disagrees with prod", not "wrong".
