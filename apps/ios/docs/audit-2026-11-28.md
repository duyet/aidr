## Across all lanes

1. **P1. Dark screenshots are light.** Mean luminance of the "dark" files equals the light files (235 vs 235 for `story-top-dark-en` / `story-top-en`; 244 for `brief-cover-dark-en`; 236 for `archive-calendar-dark`; 238 and 240 for `today-dark-*`). `story-top-dark-en` and `story-top-en` differ only in the clock region. `story-share-card-dark-en` and the light card are byte-identical (SHA 8553952a7d earlier run). The only real dark capture is `settings-dark-vi` (luminance 38). So dark mode is unverified on Today, Story, Calendar and Brief. Fix: force the appearance the same way the Settings journey does, and add a CI check that fails when a file named `dark` has mean luminance above 80.
2. **P2. Duplicate captures.** `story-actions-en` equals `story-summary-en` and `story-actions-vi` equals `story-summary-vi` (identical bytes in run b6b70c78). Capture a different state or drop the duplicate.
3. **P3. Failed UI tests still publish screenshots.** Today, Archive and Settings (newer) went red and uploaded partial sets. A red run with a partial set looks like coverage. Mark the artifact name with the result or fail the upload step.

## Today (feat/ios-today)

1. **P1. The masthead wordmark is clipped.** Every Today shot shows the yellow mark followed by a cut-off "A" before the Aa button (`today-top-en`, `today-top-vi`, `today-dark-top-en`). Fix: give the wordmark `layoutPriority(1)` and `lineLimit(1)`, or drop the app name from the bar and use the date as the title (HIG toolbars: do not title with the app name).
2. **P1. The first headline starts at about 52% of the screen height** (`today-top-en`). Above it: masthead, search field, date title with count line, category chips, trending chips, a large "28" date block, a layout toggle and a count toggle. Fix: keep the date title, the freshness line and one chip row. Move layout and count into a toolbar menu and trending into search suggestions. Target: first headline in the top third.
3. **P2. Two icon-only control groups with no labels.** The layout switch shows a picture icon, "=" and "≡", which read as the same thing; the count switch shows "8 12 16" with no word. Fix: one `Menu` with titled items, and VoiceOver labels.
4. **P2. Thumbnails are cropped link cards and one is black.** In `today-dark-ranked-en` the first row is a solid black tile; rows 2 and 3 show half a headline from a centre-cropped preview card ("eek V4 Pro 081", "re Proposes 30-Month"). Fix per DESIGN.md §2.5: 16:9 box, no centre crop, a category-coloured placeholder when an image fails.
5. **P2. The search field is a custom in-content capsule** that takes about 100 pt above the date. Fix: `.searchable` with a Search tab role, per HIG searching.
6. **P2. Freshness is correct today by luck.** The line "36 stories · updated 1m ago" (and the offline banner's separate text in `today-offline`) is driven by `feed.updatedDate` (TodayView.swift:118), which is the server's response time. It reads right only because the server stamps `Date.now()` per response. Fix: use the client's `validatedAt` from ContentCache (DESIGN.md §2.8) and fold the time into the banner: "Offline · Saved 2h ago".
7. **P3. No read state.** In `today-dark-ranked-en` every row has the same weight. Fix: dim read titles (DESIGN.md §5).
8. **P3. The Aa button opens a "Settings, Coming soon" stub** (`today-settings-en`). Expected until the Settings lane lands. Do not ship the stub.
9. Keep: the offline banner is a quiet single row with a clear icon (`today-offline`), and the Vietnamese search placeholder "Tìm trong tin đã tải" says what is searched.

## Story and share (feat/ios-story-verify)

1. **P1. Body text scrolls under the status bar and the floating buttons.** In `story-summary-en` the clock "12:30" sits on top of "approximately 6.5% of members", and the back and Aa buttons cover words. The same collision shows behind the share sheet (`story-share-composer-story-en`: "were12:31removed"). Fix: use the system navigation bar or add the system scroll edge effect and a top safe-area inset, so content never runs under the clock (HIG toolbars, scrollEdgeEffectStyle).
2. **P1. The hero image is centre-cropped and clips its own text.** `story-top-en` and `story-top-vi` show "arketBrief", "laude AI Silently Blocked Skin-" and "ketbrief.now" cut at both edges, and the image repeats the headline directly above it. The 9:16 export (`story-share-card-9x16-en`) clips the same way ("rketBrief", "aude AI Silently Blocked"). Fix: show the image at its own aspect ratio (DESIGN.md §2.5); skip the hero when it only repeats the title.
3. **P1. Dark variants are light** (see cross-lane item 1).
4. **P2. The share card crops and truncates.** In `story-share-card-en` (4:5) the embedded image is cut top and bottom ("MarketBrief" is half visible), and the summary ends "despi…" mid-word. 9:16 ends "unrelat…". Fix: render the card at the output size; end on a sentence or a whole word. The earlier run (b6b70c78) clipped the 9:16 preview at the right edge; the newer run fixes that.
5. **P2. Five competing actions.** `story-sources-en` stacks Read the source, Copy AI;DR, Share, Share as image, Suggest a story. Fix: Read the source as the one primary button; one Share menu holding link, copy, image; move Suggest a story to Settings.
6. **P2. Offline gives no sign.** `story-offline-en` looks the same as `story-top-en`. Fix: a quiet "Saved copy" tag (DESIGN.md §2.8).
7. **P3. Highlight colours differ by screen.** "6.5%" is green in the story summary and orange on Today. Confirm this is intended meaning by topic and not by position before the next release.
8. Keep: the single "Read the source" primary button, sources list with quotes and the "+ 3 more" row, the share composer's padding, radius and shadow sliders.

## Settings (feat/ios-settings)

1. **P2. Missing states.** `settings-main-en` and `settings-links-en` show no notification permission state, no "Open Settings" path when denied, no background refresh hint, no "Last checked", no stored size, no Clear cache. Fix: the sections in DESIGN.md §4 Settings.
2. **P2. The switch track is low contrast.** The yellow ON track against the white card measures 1.6:1 (sampled from `settings-main-en`); the knob also has a yellow outline. Fix: a darker on-colour or a visible border; the on-state must not rely on hue alone.
3. **P3. Marketing copy inside the Text size card.** "One short AI briefing every morning, read in a few minutes." sits under the Text size row (`settings-main-en`, `settings-main-vi`). Fix: if it is a preview, label it Preview and show a sample paragraph at the chosen size.
4. **P3. "Send a test now" is a normal row for every user.** Fix: debug builds, or only when notifications are authorized.
5. **P3. No accessibility-size capture.** `settings-large-en` is "Extra large". System size follows up to AX5 in code, but nothing shows AX3.
6. Keep: `settings-dark-vi` is a real dark render; honest notification footer ("fetched when you open the app, or when iPhone refreshes it in the background"); both languages complete.

## Brief (feat/ios-brief)

1. **P1. White text over the image, and a white Aa on a white button.** In `brief-story-en` (run 48075083) the Aa circle is white with a white glyph, so it cannot be read; the image's own text ("What is happening in markets today?") runs under the progress bar and clock; headline glyphs from the image sit behind the summary. In the earlier run (a5319531, `brief-story-en`) the "01/08" counter measures 1.4:1 against the image and the white clock about 3.5 to 4:1. Fix: a top and bottom scrim, a heavy blur of the image, a dark colour scheme for the chrome, and a gradient when the image is a text card.
2. **P1. The video page is a black rectangle and a stale video.** `brief-video-playing-en` shows an empty black player (the earlier run showed a YouTube "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" wall). The video is "October 16, 2026" inside the 28 Nov brief. The file name says "playing", which this does not show. Fix: show a video only when its date equals the edition date; use a poster image plus "Watch on YouTube" when the player cannot load.
3. **P2. Streak with a flame.** `brief-end-en` and `brief-end-vi` show a flame, "3 day streak" and a week of dots. Fix: DESIGN.md §5, "Read 3 of the last 7 days", no flame.
4. **P2. The tab bar disappears on story pages** and the only way back is an unlabeled list icon bottom-left (`brief-story-en`). Fix: present the brief as a full-screen cover with an explicit Close, or keep the tab bar (HIG tab bars).
5. **P2. Dark variants are light** (cross-lane item 1).
6. **P3. The cover headline is cut at "dating app's..."** while three lines are free (`brief-cover-en`). The cover and end pages leave the top fifth empty.
7. **P3. The end page has no freshness or "next edition" line** (`brief-end-en`).
8. Keep: the finite "You're all caught up" end, the cover with date, count and reading time, the clean gradient page (`brief-story-plain-en`).
