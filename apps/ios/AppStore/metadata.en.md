## Description
AI;DR reads the day's AI news and gives you one ranked edition. It comes in English and Vietnamese.

Open the app and see today's top stories first. Each story has a short summary written by AI;DR and a link to the original source. There is nothing to sign in to.

What is in the app
• Today: the day's stories in ranked order, with topic filters and search.
• Digest: the whole day in 8, 12 or 16 short lines.
• Archive: a calendar and a weekly list of every past edition.
• Offline: the last edition is saved on your iPhone, so the app opens fast and still reads without a connection.
• Daily reminder: pick a time and get one notification with the top headline and its picture.
• Share: send a story link, or turn a story into an image card with your own background, padding and rounded corners.
• Explain simply and Why it matters: on an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, the app explains a story in plain words. This runs on your device. The story text is not sent anywhere.

Your privacy
No account. No ads. The app does not track you across other apps and websites. Your language, text size and reminder time stay on your iPhone.

About the content
AI;DR picks and summarizes stories with software, so it can make mistakes. For anything that matters, read the original. Headlines and pictures belong to their publishers. Every story links to the place where it was first published.

The Apple Intelligence features need a supported iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, iOS 26 or later, and a supported language. If your iPhone does not meet this, the rest of the app works the same.
