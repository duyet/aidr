### Title

AI;DR v0.1.18 — Duplicate stories merge again

### Description

```
Since Oct 26, copies of the same story could appear as separate items on AI;DR. The first model that groups stories had started to answer in plain sentences, and that answer stopped the chain. Now the next model takes over, and copies of one story are grouped again. Scoring, translation repair, captions and the email summary follow the same rule.
```
