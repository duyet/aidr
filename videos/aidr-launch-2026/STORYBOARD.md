# Storyboard

76 s at 30 fps. Music at 120 BPM: a beat every 0.5 s, a bar accent on every even second. Cuts sit on the beat; most on an even second. Voice lines start at the times below; word times come from `assets/voice/*.words.json`, and visual hits key off them in `scripts/build.mjs` (`word(line, n)`).

Ground is paper `#F7F7F5` unless noted; one yellow `#F5C518` accent per frame; EB Garamond for headlines, Source Sans 3 for labels, monospace for data. Every shot has one linear root push (2–4 %) from t=0.
