## Intent

The 2026 launch film for https://aidr.today. Owner request (2026-10-21): "a NEW, very impressive aidr.today launch video". Style is the current AI;DR design system (`videos/brand`, the live site) mixed with anatomy-style isometric technical illustration: the pipeline drawn as a physical machine, with art-directed motion design.

- Real footage and assets: captures of the live site (home, a story, /data, /subscribe, /about), the email preview, the public English Telegram channel, a day card, and daily-video thumbnails, all pulled read-only on 2026-10-21 by `scripts/capture.mjs` into the ignored `capture/` folder.
- Sound: a music bed, effects whose peak lands within one frame of the visual hit, and an English voiceover with a short hook first and no sentence over 18 words.
- Every product claim comes from `docs/marketing/positioning.md` and `apps/web/ALGORITHM.md`. The claim-by-claim map is `data/claims.md`. No invented numbers.

## Deliverables

- 16:9: this folder, authored at 1920x1080, rendered at 3840x2160 for YouTube (long).
- 9:16: `../aidr-launch-2026-9x16/`, the same timeline and audio laid out again for Reels and Shorts, rendered at 2160x3840.
- A 4K cover still for each (`renders/*-cover.png`).

Both `index.html` files are generated by `scripts/build.mjs` from one scene list, so timing, copy and audio cannot drift between the cuts.

## Assets

- `assets/machine.svg`, `assets/machine.json`: the isometric machine, built by `machine/build.mjs` with the anatomy kit (MIT, vendored in `machine/kit/`).
- `assets/voice/`: ElevenLabs voiceover from `data/script.json` (`scripts/voice.mjs`), hosts Alex and Allison from `videos/brand/voices.json`.
- `assets/bgm/`: "News Theme" (the library track the first launch film used), 120 BPM, entered 1.5 s in so its bar accents land on even seconds.
- `assets/sfx/`: from the media-use effects library.
- `data/`: today's edition (`public-2026-10-21.json`), source list (`sources-2026-10-21.json`), the script, and the claims sheet.

## Notes

- The name is written "AI DR" in the voice text so the engine reads four quick letters (first launch film's note).
- YouTube is drawn as a branch fed from the finished edition, not as a port of the Worker: the daily video is made by the daily-news pipeline, not by the ingest run.
- Avoid list from `videos/brand/brand.md`: dark gradient glow, particles, purple-to-blue gradients, bouncy eases.
