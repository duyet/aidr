Inter is the default for headlines and body, and it already ships. Source Serif 4 is optional in Customize and loads only when picked, so the default build adds no font.

Color tokens (same names as packages/ui/tokens.css)

Contrast is computed in this page from the hex values. Yellow stays a mark and a dark-mode accent. On light surfaces text uses the brown accent.

Buttons

Three styles. Primary is the one filled action on a screen. Secondary is a soft fill with no border. A link is colored text, and most secondary actions (copy, share, show more, source) are links, not buttons. Every button is 32px high, 13px text, fully rounded, with the same focus ring. The header AI;DR button and the AI;DR tab use the logo mark as their icon.

Link

Icons

One set, 15px, 2px stroke, round caps, always next to a label except the layout switch and the header Aa.

Chips, switches, the search box and inputs use the same soft fill, so the only borders left are focus rings and the selected color swatch.

Topic colors

One fixed color per category. It colors key names in the text, colors the dot on each row and chip, and always sits next to the category label, so color is never the only signal. Highlights can also be one color or off. Text uses a darker shade in light mode so it keeps 4.5:1 on every surface.

Validator result: nine hues fail the palette checks. Light mode: chroma floor (Agents, Industry, Data read gray-ish) and separation (Models vs Chips, ΔE 1.4 for deutan; Agents vs Funding, ΔE 7.8). Dark mode: separation and lightness band. Labels always sit beside the color, which the validator allows for the 6 to 8 CVD band but not for the normal-vision floor. Before shipping, cut to 7 topics or re-step teal against green and blue against violet.

For production, define these as hex plus alpha tokens. DESIGN.md warns that color-mix can compile to invalid oklch with LightningCSS.

The default follows the system. Dark is its own palette. Panels lift by lightness, not by shadow. The accent changes from brown to yellow. Heat cells shift to a dark amber ramp.

Layouts, language and customization

Compact (default)15px titles, 44px thumbs, 7px row padding. Titles wrap to two lines at about 59px a row. The live site shows 29px rows with titles cut off, so compact trades some density for full titles.
NormalRoomier rows, 64px thumbs, one-line summaries available.
NewspaperMasthead with double rule, serif headlines, justified text, drop cap, 2 or 3 ruled columns, square corners.
VI + ENBoth languages in one view. The leading language is full size; the other sits under it, smaller and muted. EN and VI keep separate text, never falling back.

The customize drawer sets quick looks, layout, language and which language leads, mode, background, one of five accents, headline and body fonts, size, newspaper columns and five section toggles. It saves to this browser.

Shape, spacing, motion, dialogs

The base unit is 4px, with gaps of 8, 12, 16, 24 and 32. Content is 1100px wide, the reading column 680px and the rail 300px. Panels have a 14px radius, images 10, thumbs 8 and pills 999. One hairline, no shadows except on layers.

Motion is 150ms ease-out for hover and chips and 220ms for layers. It turns off under reduced motion.

A story opens as a 520px side panel on desktop and a bottom sheet on phone, with the page behind still scrollable. Preferences, search and menus are small popovers. Every layer closes with Esc and returns focus to its trigger.

    AI;DR là gìWhat AI;DR is

    Mỗi 30 phút, aidr thu tin AI, chấm điểm và xếp hạng thành một số báo bằng tiếng Anh và tiếng Việt. Không ai chọn thứ tự bằng tay.Every 30 minutes aidr collects AI news, scores it and ranks it into one edition in English and Vietnamese. No person picks the order.

    1 Lịch chạy1 Schedulemỗi 30 phútevery 30 min
    2 Thu thập2 Fetchloại trùng theo URLdedupe by URL
    3 Chấm điểm3 ScoreJEV, rồi LLMJEV, then LLM
    4 Xếp hạng4 Rank72 giờ gần nhấtlast 72h

    6 Gửi6 Sendemail · Telegram · FBmail · Telegram · FB
